# Bike service booking

1. Customer books online or in store; the booking lands in the [[Workshop scheduler]].
2. A [[Mechanic]] checks the bike in and records the job.
3. Parts are reserved in the [[Inventory system]].
4. On completion the customer is texted and pays through [[Point of sale]].

Owner: [[Head mechanic]]. E-bikes go through [[E-bike diagnostics]] first.
